## Direction contract

THESIS: Paris's own 1900s Métro ironwork, alive and moving — refusing the near-black neon-dot SaaS dashboard every "live tracker" defaults to.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black night ground; verdigris (#4a6b57) and bronze (#a9823f) ironwork linework; Guimard lamp-globe station glyphs with amber (#e0a339) glow. One earned accent, warm red (#c23b2d), reserved for live disruptions only. Position certainty is drawn, not colored: confirmed GPS is a solid glowing filament, predicted-only position is a fine dashed filament; a disrupted segment also thickens and pulses, not just recolors.

STORY: The visitor understands the network is alive right now — quiet verdigris where it runs smoothly, pulsing amber/red where it's disrupted. Nothing to complete; they watch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark map fills the viewport instantly with static ironwork geometry; train glyphs on live-tracked lines fade in individually as WebSocket data arrives. Disruption ticker is a slim overlay, never a floating glass panel.

FORM: Guimard's Ironwork — assigned index 6 of 7 self-derived candidates; seed key 8fd1e67e.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
